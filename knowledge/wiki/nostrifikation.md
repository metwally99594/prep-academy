# Nostrifikation — Medical Degree Recognition in Austria

## Overview

Nostrifikation is the formal recognition of a foreign medical degree in Austria, required for physicians who obtained their qualification outside the EU/EEA before they can practice. The process is governed by the **Ärztegesetz 1998 (ÄrzteG)** and administered by the **Bundesministerium für Soziales, Gesundheit, Pflege und Konsumentenschutz**.

## Requirements

- Diploma and transcript evaluation (translated to German by certified translator)
- Proof of German language proficiency (C1 level, often ÖSD or Goethe certificate)
- **Stichprobentest** (sample examination) at the relevant medical university
- Certificate of good standing from home country medical board
- Proof of identity and citizenship/residence permit

## Stichprobentest

A practical-oral examination administered by the medical university in the applicant's state of residence. Tests competence across the major clinical disciplines. The scope corresponds to Austrian medical graduate standards.

## After Recognition

Once the Nostrifikation is granted:

1. **Austrian Medical License (Ärztliches Diplom)** — enables application for the **Kenntnisprüfung (KP)**
2. **Kenntnisprüfung** — knowledge examination covering the full medical curriculum (see [KP Vorbereitung](sources/kp-vorbereitung-amboss.md))
3. If KP is passed → **turnusärztliche Tätigkeit** (residency) in Austria
4. After residency → **Fachärztliche Prüfung** (board certification) in chosen specialty
