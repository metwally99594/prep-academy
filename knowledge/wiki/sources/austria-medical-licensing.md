# Austria Medical Licensing

**Source:** `knowledge/raw/Austria_Medical_Licensing.txt`

## Summary

Three key facts about medical degree recognition in Austria:

1. **Foreign Medical Degree Recognition** — Foreign-trained physicians must have their degree recognized by the Austrian Ministry of Health (Bundesministerium für Soziales, Gesundheit, Pflege und Konsumentenschutz). The process is called **Nostrifikation**.

2. **Stichprobentest** — The recognition process typically requires a sample examination (Stichprobentest) administered by the medical university in the applicant's state of residence. This tests competence in the relevant medical field.

3. **Legal Basis** — The procedure is governed by the Ärztegesetz 1998 (ÄrzteG), specifically regulations concerning the recognition of foreign medical diplomas.
