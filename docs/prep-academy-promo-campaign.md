# Prep Academy Promotional Campaign

## Campaign Positioning

Prep Academy is a focused medical exam preparation platform for German-speaking medical licensing and readiness workflows. The strongest message is not generic AI hype. It is precision, structure, and confidence for doctors preparing under pressure.

## Main Ad Copy

Headline:
PrepAcademy - klar vorbereitet. Sicherer bestehen.

Primary text:
Trainiere medizinische Pruefungsfragen, erkenne deine Schwachstellen und lerne mit KI-gestuetzter Erklaerung, Quellen und strukturiertem Fortschritt. PrepAcademy verbindet Quiz, Lernanalyse, Tutor und medizinische Wissenssuche in einer Plattform.

CTA:
Kostenlos testen

Short version:
Medizin lernen mit System. Fragen trainieren, Fehler verstehen, Fortschritt sehen. PrepAcademy bringt KI, Quiz und Wissenssuche in einen klaren Lernflow.

## Visual Direction

- Clean medical premium style.
- Real interface footage over abstract stock visuals.
- Use desktop and mobile shots.
- Keep typography sharp and calm.
- Avoid exaggerated AI visuals; show practical workflows.

## Automation Recommendation

Automation is needed before serious scale.

Recommended automations:

1. CI checks on every push: backend tests, frontend build, syntax checks.
2. Post-deploy smoke test for Render and Vercel.
3. Scheduled RAG benchmark with Recall@5, Recall@10, MRR, and latency.
4. Uptime and endpoint monitoring.
5. Obsidian sync monitor and failure alert.
6. Visual regression check for homepage, quiz, login, admin, and mobile views.
7. Sentry or equivalent production error monitoring.

The platform can operate now without heavy automation, but reaching true 100% production maturity requires these automations.
